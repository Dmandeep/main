## Actionable takeaways for IdeaSpace
1) Motion should communicate progress.
   GSAP highlights purposeful motion. Use subtle reveal and confirmation motion
   for health score updates, RSVP confirmations, and proof verification.

2) Make flows visible, not implied.
   Pageflows and Mobbin emphasize complete journeys. IdeaSpace should surface
   quick "next step" states on idea cards and workshops.

3) Build a living pattern library.
   Refero and 21st.dev show value in categorized UI. Keep IdeaSpace components
   named and discoverable (Idea Card, Proof Rail, RSVP Module).

4) Use crisp technical blocks for credibility.
   Modal and Antimetal use clean, structured sections and metrics. Use this on
   landing and proof wall to avoid "student project" vibes.

5) Activity feeds create trust.
   Antimetal shows recent merges. IdeaSpace should show real build updates,
   not vanity stats, on the wall and idea detail.

6) Landing pages should be modular and fast.
   Framer templates favor strong hero, feature blocks, and social proof.
   Use a proof-first hero and short sections.

## Design system adjustments from wave 1
- Emphasize the Proof Rail as a signature UI element.
- Add a "flow state" chip on idea cards (e.g., DISCOVERY, BUILDING, SHIPPED).
- Include a compact activity list on feed and profile.
- Keep a clear split between content, actions, and evidence.

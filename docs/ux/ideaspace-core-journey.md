# User Journey: Post an Idea to First Collaborator

## User persona
- Who: Student builder with a half-baked idea and urgency to validate it
- Goal: post an idea, see if anyone cares, recruit a collaborator
- Context: between classes, time-constrained, wants clarity
- Success metric: one serious collaborator within 48 hours

## Stage 1: Awareness
What the user does: hears about IdeaSpace from a club or friend
What they think: "Is this another empty platform or does it actually work?"
What they feel: skeptical but curious
Pain points:
- Too many tools promise visibility but deliver nothing
Opportunity:
- Proof-first landing with real student projects and activity

## Stage 2: Exploration
What the user does: scans feed, filters by tags, checks proof wall
What they think: "Are these ideas legit and active?"
What they feel: cautious, looking for signals
Pain points:
- Hard to tell if an idea is dead or active
Opportunity:
- Flow state chips and last activity on every card

## Stage 3: Action
What the user does: posts an idea using guided form
What they think: "Am I describing this well enough?"
What they feel: focused, wants quick feedback
Pain points:
- Overly long forms kill momentum
Opportunity:
- Health score with 3 targeted suggestions and live preview

## Stage 4: Outcome
What the user does: receives collaborator request and responds
What they think: "Finally, someone serious."
What they feel: validated and motivated
Success metrics:
- Collaborator request within 48 hours
- First update posted within a week

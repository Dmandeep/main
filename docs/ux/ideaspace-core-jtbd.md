# IdeaSpace JTBD (Core)

## User persona
- Who: Student builder (2nd-4th year), some project experience, wants to ship
- Skill level: Intermediate with tools and GitHub, time-constrained
- Primary device: Mobile for discovery, desktop for writing and proof

## Job statement
When I have an idea or want to join a project, I want to quickly evaluate and commit to the right build with clear expectations and evidence, so I can ship real work and earn trust without wasting time.

## Context and triggers
- New semester, hackathon, or club push
- Looking for teammates or mentors
- Need to prove activity for placements or portfolio

## Current solution and pain points
- Current: WhatsApp groups, Google Forms, informal club chats
- Pain: scattered, no ownership clarity, no proof, easy to ghost
- Consequence: good ideas die and contributions are invisible

## Desired outcomes
- Find a valid project path in under 10 minutes
- Post an idea in under 5 minutes with a clear health score
- Get first collaborator within 48 hours

## Success metrics
- Onboarding to first action in under 5 minutes
- 60 percent of posted ideas get at least one collaborator
- Proof wall entries per active user increase month over month

## Assumptions to validate
- Students are comfortable with short, guided forms
- Visibility and proof matter more than long descriptions
- Mobile discovery is the top entry point
